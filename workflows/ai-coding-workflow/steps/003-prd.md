# 003 - PRD

**Skill name:** `prd`

**Step type:** Core workflow step (required, except for trivial changes).

**Role:** Define the product and functional behavior that future technical design, slicing, and implementation must satisfy.

**When to use:** Before any non-trivial implementation. Detail level should match scope, risk, and ambiguity.

**Possible inputs:** Clear idea, `brief.md`, `validation.md`, grill summary, user feedback, product constraints, project context, existing documentation, relevant current behavior.

**Process:**

1. Choose PRD size before writing.
2. Consolidate useful context from brief, validation, user feedback, and existing docs.
3. Clarify problem, goals, users, use cases, and target product behavior.
4. Define observable requirements, edge cases, states, permissions, and errors at product level.
5. Separate current scope, non-goals, and future candidates.
6. Capture product constraints, success signals, decisions, assumptions, and open questions when useful.
7. Route unresolved technical decisions to `Tech Design` and unresolved product judgment to `Grill Me` or `Grill With Docs`.

**Rules:**

- PRD is product truth, not technical design.
- Stack choices, architecture, data model, endpoints, migrations, and test strategy belong in `Tech Design` or `Slice`.
- Use durable vocabulary from `CONTEXT.md` or project docs when available.
- State assumptions when context is incomplete.
- Requirements must be concrete, observable, and sliceable.

**Output:** Markdown PRD that becomes the current product source for `Tech Design`, `Slice`, or a small direct `Build`.

**Output location:** Check `.agents/workflow/output-locations.md` when it exists. Recommended default: parent GitHub/tracker issue body. Local Markdown alternative: `.initiatives/<initiative>/prd.md`.

**Output template:**

Use the smallest useful PRD and omit sections without real signal.

```markdown
# <Initiative or Feature Name>

## Summary
<!-- Required. Paragraph, 1-3 sentences: what is being built, for whom, and why. -->
<Concise product summary.>

## Problem
<!-- Required. Paragraph, 2-5 sentences: user or business problem, not the absence of a solution. -->
<Problem statement.>

## Goals
<!-- Required. Bullet list: product, user, or business outcomes. Avoid feature lists here. -->
- <Outcome the product should achieve.>

## Users & Use Cases
<!-- Required. Bullet list: solution-agnostic actor needs and context. -->
- **<Actor or segment>:** <Need, context, or job-to-be-done.>

## Current Behavior
<!-- Conditional. Paragraph or bullets: include for existing behavior, UX changes, migrations, or bug-like product changes. Omit for standalone new behavior. -->
<Current journey, limitation, or behavior that will change.>

## Proposed Product Behavior
<!-- Required. Paragraph, 2-6 sentences: target observable behavior from the user's perspective. -->
<Target product behavior.>

## Conceptual Model / Vocabulary
<!-- Conditional. Definition list: include when roles, states, permissions, domain objects, or terms could be misunderstood. -->
- **<Term>:** <Definition.>

## Requirements
<!-- Required. Grouped bullet lists. Each item must be concrete, observable, and sliceable product behavior. -->
<!-- Use P0/P1/P2 only when prioritization helps. Omit priority labels for small PRDs where all requirements are equally required. -->
<!-- P0 = required for MVP/release. P1 = important but not release-blocking. P2 = nice-to-have or deferred candidate. -->

### <Journey or Capability>
- [P0] <Required user-visible behavior or product rule.>
- [P0] If <edge case or error state>, <expected product behavior.>
- [P1] <Important non-blocking behavior.>
- [P2] <Optional enhancement or deferred candidate.>

## Scope
<!-- Required. Boundary section. Use it for exclusions and deferred candidates, not as a duplicate feature list. -->

### Out of Scope
<!-- Required. Bullet list: explicit non-goals and excluded behavior. -->
- <Explicit non-goal or excluded behavior.>

### Future Candidates
<!-- Conditional. Bullet list: useful deferred ideas that are not current scope. -->
- <Deferred idea that should not be mistaken for current scope.>

## Product Constraints
<!-- Conditional. Bullet list: product-level constraints that affect behavior or slicing. -->
- **Accessibility:** <User-visible accessibility requirement.>
- **Privacy:** <User data, consent, retention, or visibility constraint.>
- **Compatibility:** <Supported device, browser, platform, locale, or mode.>
- **Performance perception:** <User-visible responsiveness expectation.>

## Success Signals
<!-- Conditional. Bullet list: metrics, qualitative signals, or outcomes to observe. -->
- <Metric, qualitative signal, or product outcome to observe.>

## Decisions, Assumptions & Open Questions
<!-- Conditional. Include only meaningful items. Omit empty subsections. -->

### Decisions
<!-- Bullet list. -->
- <Accepted product decision.>

### Assumptions
<!-- Bullet list. -->
- <Assumption that may need validation later.>

### Open Questions
<!-- Bullet list. Mark blocking questions explicitly when needed. -->
- <Blocking or non-blocking product question.>

## References
<!-- Conditional. Bullet list: links or source names, not copied source text. -->
- <Brief, validation notes, mocks, research, issue, customer feedback, or durable docs.>
```

**Possible sizes:** Minimal PRD for a small, clear behavior change; standard PRD for a multi-flow feature; full PRD for a product-critical, cross-functional, or high-uncertainty initiative.

**Verification:** A reviewer can determine included behavior, excluded behavior, unresolved product questions, constraints, and sliceability without reading prior chat.

**Human gate:** Validate scope, ambiguous behaviors, requirements, and important product decisions.
